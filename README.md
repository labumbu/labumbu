# Yegor Isaev

Co-founder of [Quelta](https://quelta.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=labumbu): one click turns product photos, or one existing ad, into video ads for every market you sell in, localized by meaning. Before anything runs, each ad gets a 0–100 score for that country and audience (does the hook stop the scroll, is the on-screen text in the buyer's language, are the brand and price clear), and the weak hook, text or visuals get fixed.
